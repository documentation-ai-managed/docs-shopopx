## 1. Dashboard and overview

The dashboard is the central hub for at-a-glance information and quick navigation.

- **Dashboard**: Displays key performance indicators (KPIs) and summaries of business activities.

- **Welcome message**: Personalized greeting and high-level account summary.

- **Renew list widget**: Summary view of customers whose services or products are due for renewal.

- **Lead listing widget**: Quick view of recent or open leads.

- **Invoice list widget**: Summary of recent or pending invoices.

- **Technician overview widget**: Snapshot of technician status or workload.

- **Schedule and upcoming works**: Calendar view showing scheduled tasks and a list of upcoming jobs.

- **Business analytics widget**: Visual charts showing statistics for maintenance, warranty, and installation cases:

  - Total renewed warranty amount

  - Total installation amount

  - Total maintenance

  - Pending (not renewed yet) warranty amount

  - Cancelled (confirmed not renewed) warranty amount

---

## 2. Quick actions

Quick actions provide shortcuts for creating new records directly from the main interface.

- Add customer

- Add lead

- Add installation

- Add maintenance or scheduler

- Add product

---

## 3. Lead management

Lead management tools support the entire lead lifecycle, from capture to conversion.

- **Lead list**: View, search, filter, and manage all leads.

- **Add lead**: Dedicated form to create a new lead record.

---

## 4. Customer management

Customer management is the core module for maintaining all customer information.

- **Customer list**: Comprehensive database of all active customers with search and filter capabilities.

- **Add customer**: Form to create a new customer profile.

- **Archive list**: List of inactive or archived customers, keeping the main list clean while retaining data.

---

## 5. Product management

Product management covers the company's product and service catalog.

- **Product list**: View and manage all products or services offered, including details like SKU and pricing.

- **Add product**: Interface to add new items to the product catalog.

---

## 6. Service and case management

Service and case management modules track all service-related activities.

### Installation

- **Installation case list**: Track the status of all product installation jobs.

- **Add installation**: Create a new installation case for a customer.

### Maintenance

- **Maintenance case list**: Manage all scheduled and ad-hoc maintenance tasks.

- **Add maintenance**: Create a new maintenance request or job.

### Warranty

- **Warranty list**: View and track warranty status for customers and their products.

---

## 7. Scheduling and workforce management

Scheduling and workforce management tools organize fieldwork and manage internal teams.

- **Work scheduler**: Detailed calendar or list view for assigning and tracking all scheduled tasks, including installations and maintenance.

### Technician management

- **Technician list**: Manage the profiles and schedules of all service technicians.

- **Add technician**: Onboard a new technician into the system.

### Sales person management

- **Sales person list**: Manage profiles and performance of the sales team.

- **Add sales person**: Add a new salesperson to the system.

---

## 8. Reporting and finance

Reporting and finance tools generate critical business documents and performance insights.

- **Invoice generation**: Create and view invoices for services and products.

- **Receipt generation**: Create and track payment receipts.

- **Performance reports**: Generate reports on installation and maintenance activities to analyze efficiency and workload.

---

## 9. Company management

Company management supports B2B scenarios by managing client organizations.

- **Company list**: Manage a list of business clients or partner companies.

- **Add company**: Create a new company profile, which you can link to multiple individual customer contacts.

---

## 10. System settings

System settings provide administrative controls for configuration and user management.

### User management

- **User list**: Manage all system users, their roles, and permissions.

- **Add user**: Create accounts for new employees.

### Parameter management

- **Parameter list**: Configure system-wide parameters such as dropdown options, categories, and other customizable variables.

- **Add parameter**: Define new parameters to customize system behavior.
