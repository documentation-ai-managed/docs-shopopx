---
title: 'Bio-Link CRM System: Functions and Features'
description: >
  1. Dashboard & Overview 

  The central hub for at-a-glance information and quick navigation.



  Dashboard: Displays key performance indicators (KPIs) and summaries of
  business activities.

  Welcome Message: Personalized greeting and high-level account summary.

  Renew List Widget: A summary view of customers whose services or products are
  due for renewal.

  Lead Listing Widget: A quick view of recent or open leads.

  Invoice List Widget: A summary of recent or pending invoices.

  Technician Overview Widget: Provides a snapshot of technician status or
  workload.

  Schedule & Upcoming Works: A calendar view showing scheduled tasks and a list
  of upcoming jobs.

  Business Analytics Widget: Visual charts showing statistics for maintenance,
  warranty, and installation cases.
   - Total renewed warranty amount 
   - Total installation amount 
   - Total maintenance 
   - Pending (not renewed yet) warranty amount 
   - Cancelled (confirmed not renewed) warranty amount 

  ---


  2. Quick Actions

  Shortcuts for creating new records directly from the main interface.


  Add Customer

  Add Lead

  Add Installation

  Add Maintenance/Scheduler

  Add Product


  ---


  3. Lead Management

  Tools for managing the entire lead lifecycle, from capture to conversion.


  Lead List: View, search, filter, and manage all leads.

  Add Lead: A dedicated form to create a new lead record.


  ---


  4. Customer Management 

  Core module for maintaining all customer information.


  Customer List: A comprehensive database of all active customers with search
  and filter capabilities.

  Add Customer: Form to create a new customer profile.

  Archive List: A list of inactive or archived customers, keeping the main list
  clean while retaining data.


  ---


  5. Product Management

  Manages the company's product and service catalog.


  Product List : View and manage all products or services offered, including
  details like SKU and pricing.

  Add Product : Interface to add new items to the product catalog.


  ---


  6. Service & Case Management

  Modules for tracking all service-related activities.


  Installation 
      Installation Case List : Track the status of all product installation jobs.
      Add Installation : Create a new installation case for a customer.

  Maintenance 
      Maintenance Case List : Manage all scheduled and ad-hoc maintenance tasks.
      Add Maintenance : Create a new maintenance request or job.

  Warranty
      Warranty List : View and track warranty status for customers and their products.

  ---


  7. Scheduling & Workforce Management 

  Tools to organize fieldwork and manage internal teams.


  Work Scheduler : A detailed calendar or list view for assigning and tracking
  all scheduled tasks (installations, maintenance, etc.).


  Technician Management 
      Technician List : Manage the profiles and schedules of all service technicians.
      Add Technician : Onboard a new technician into the system.

  Sales Person Management 
      Sales Person List : Manage profiles and performance of the sales team.
      Add Sales Person : Add a new salesperson to the system.

  ---


  8. Reporting & Finance 

  Generate critical business documents and reports.



  Invoice Generation : Create and view invoices for services and products.

  Receipt Generation : Create and track payment receipts.

  Performance Reports : Generate reports on installation and maintenance
  activities to analyze efficiency and workload.


  ---


  9. Company Management 

  For B2B scenarios, manage client organizations.


  Company List : Manage a list of business clients or partner companies.

  Add Company : Create a new company profile, which can be linked to multiple
  individual customer contacts.


  ---


  10. System Settings 

  Administrative controls for system configuration and user management.


  User Management 
      User List : Manage all system users, their roles, and permissions.
      Add User : Create accounts for new employees.

  Parameter Management 
      Parameter List : Configure system-wide parameters, such as dropdown options, categories, and other customizable variables.
      Add Parameter : Define new parameters to customize system behavior.
