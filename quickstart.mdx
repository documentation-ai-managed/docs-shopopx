## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
Ensure you have:
- A business email address for account verification
- Your brand logo and colors ready (e.g., primary color `#FF8C3B`)
- Access to your retail or restaurant payment systems for integration
</Callout>

Review these requirements to avoid delays during setup.

## Create and Verify Your Account

Follow these steps to get your Shopopx account up and running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://dashboard.shopopx.com/signup` and enter your business details.

    Choose your plan (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise) based on your membership program needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification email from Shopopx.

    Click the link to activate your account. If not received, check spam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Password" icon="lock">
    Create a strong password following the guidelines shown.

    Enable two-factor authentication for added security.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure Initial Brand Settings

Customize your Shopopx instance to match your retail or restaurant brand.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard UI" icon="settings">
    Navigate to **Brand Settings** in the sidebar.

    Upload your logo, set primary color to `#FF8C3B`, and add your store locations.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API Integration" icon="code">
    Generate your API key from **Integrations > API**.

    <CodeGroup tabs="cURL,JavaScript">
      ````bash
      curl -X POST https://api.example.com/v1/brands \
        -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
        -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
        -d '{
          "name": "Your Restaurant",
          "primaryColor": "#FF8C3B",
          "logoUrl": "https://your-logo.com/logo.png"
        }'
      ````

      ````javascript
      const response = await fetch('https://api.example.com/v1/brands', {
        method: 'POST',
        headers: {
          'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY',
          'Content-Type': 'application/json',
        },
        body: JSON.stringify({
          name: 'Your Restaurant',
          primaryColor: '#FF8C3B',
          logoUrl: 'https://your-logo.com/logo.png'
        })
      });
      const brand = await response.json();
      console.log(brand);
      ````
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Set Up Your First Loyalty Program

Launch a simple points-based loyalty program for your customers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Program" icon="award">
    Go to **Loyalty > Programs** and click **New Program**.

    Name it "VIP Rewards" and set points per dollar spent (e.g., 10 points = $1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Tiers" icon="layers">
    Add tiers like Bronze (0 pts), Silver (500 pts), Gold (2000 pts).

    Assign rewards such as 5% discount for Silver members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="globe">
    Review settings and click **Publish**.

    Test by enrolling a sample customer via the API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField path="programId" param-type="string" required="true">
  Unique ID for the loyalty program.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="pointsPerDollar" param-type="number" required="false" default="10">
  Points earned per dollar spent.
</ParamField>
