title: Login Issues
description: Quick fix for Shopopx login problems.
---

## Symptoms

- Can't log in to Shopopx
- Password reset not working

## Fix Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Make sure your email and password are typed correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page to reset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try logging in after clearing your cache/cookies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If still stuck, email support@shopopx.com.
  </Step>
</Steps>
