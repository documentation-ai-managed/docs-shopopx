## Overview

Follow these steps to add a product to Shopopx:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Products">
    From your dashboard, click "Products" in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Product">
    Press the "Add Product" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter product details">
    Fill in name, price, and description. Optionally upload images.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click "Save" to list your product.
  </Step>
</Steps>
