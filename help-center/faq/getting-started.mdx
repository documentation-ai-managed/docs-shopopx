## Overview

Getting started with Shopopx is simple:

1. Go to `https://shopopx.com/register`
2. Create your account with email and password
3. Log in to your dashboard
4. Click "Create New Project"
5. Fill in your store details and save

That's it! You can now manage your project documentation and settings from your Shopopx dashboard.
