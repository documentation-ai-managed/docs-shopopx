## Overview

Shopopx provides an all-in-one membership CRM and loyalty marketing platform tailored for retail and restaurant brands. You connect in-store and online experiences through a powerful cloud-based CRM and loyalty engine, driving revenue growth with personalized customer journeys. Manage memberships, track customer data, and launch targeted campaigns from a single dashboard.
