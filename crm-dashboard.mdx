The dashboard is the best place to get a quick pulse on your business before drilling into detailed CRM modules like customers, leads, or invoices.

</Callout>

## Overview

The CRM dashboard is the central hub for at-a-glance information and quick navigation. Use it to see key performance indicators (KPIs), track upcoming work, and access core lists without opening each module.

Typical dashboard widgets include:

- Welcome message and account summary
- Renew list widget
- Lead listing widget
- Invoice list widget
- Technician overview widget
- Schedule and upcoming works
- Business analytics widget

Each widget summarizes recent activity or upcoming work so you can decide where to focus next.

## Welcome message and account summary

The welcome message provides a personalized greeting and a high-level overview of your account. This section helps you understand the current state of your business before you review individual widgets.

You may see highlights such as recent activity, important notices, or shortcuts to frequently used CRM areas.

## Renew list widget

The renew list widget shows customers whose services or products are due for renewal. This keeps potential revenue and customer retention tasks visible without running separate reports.

Use this widget to:

- Identify contracts or warranties that will expire soon
- Prioritize outreach to customers who are close to renewal
- Monitor which renewals are still pending vs already handled

When you see an at-risk renewal, open the full customer or contract record from the widget to follow up.

## Lead listing widget

The lead listing widget displays a quick view of recent or open leads so you can manage your sales pipeline directly from the dashboard.

Typical uses include:

- Reviewing the newest leads added to the system
- Spotting leads that have not been contacted recently
- Jumping into a lead record to update status or add notes

By starting from this widget each day, you reduce the chance of missing valuable sales opportunities.

## Invoice list widget

The invoice list widget summarizes recent or pending invoices. It helps you keep cash flow tasks visible without navigating to the invoices module.

Use the widget to:

- See newly created invoices
- Track which invoices remain unpaid or pending
- Open specific invoices to record payments or make updates

This overview supports faster follow-up on overdue or high-value invoices.

## Technician overview widget

The technician overview widget provides a snapshot of technician status or workload. This is especially useful for operations and service teams coordinating field work.

From this widget you can:

- See which technicians are currently active or assigned work
- Compare workloads across technicians at a glance
- Decide how to distribute new jobs based on current capacity

Use it alongside the schedule widget to understand both who is available and what work is planned.

## Schedule and upcoming works

The schedule and upcoming works area combines a calendar-style view with a list of upcoming jobs. It shows what is scheduled for today and the near future.

Use this widget to:

- Check today’s appointments and tasks
- Confirm technicians and teams have work assigned
- Spot gaps or conflicts in the upcoming schedule

When you click an item in the list or calendar, open the related job or work order to view details and make adjustments if needed.

## Business analytics widget

The business analytics widget displays visual charts and statistics for maintenance, warranty, and installation cases. It highlights both total amounts and renewal performance so you can monitor trends.

Key metrics include:

- **Total renewed warranty amount** – Value of warranties that have been successfully renewed.
- **Total installation amount** – Value of completed installation work.
- **Total maintenance** – Value of maintenance services delivered.
- **Pending warranty amount** – Warranty renewals that are not yet confirmed or completed.
- **Cancelled warranty amount** – Warranty renewals that are confirmed as not renewed.

Review these analytics regularly to understand where revenue comes from, how renewals are performing, and where to focus improvement efforts.

## Working with the dashboard

To get the most value from the dashboard:

- Start each day by scanning renewals, leads, invoices, and upcoming works.
- Use widgets as launch points to open detailed records when something needs action.
- Compare technician overview data with the schedule to balance workload.
- Track business analytics over time to spot growth areas or declining segments.

If a widget does not show the data you expect, verify that records in the relevant CRM module are up to date.
